Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

GeoLens ("the App," "we," "us") is an AI-powered rock identification application. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it's used, and what choices you have.

2. Information We Collect

On-Device Data (never leaves your device)

  • Photos you take of rocks/minerals for identification
  • Your identification history and saved rock collection
  • App preferences and settings

Data Sent Off-Device (via API requests)

When the App needs the AI model to identify a rock, the photo (or data extracted from it) is sent to our identification service/API to generate a result. Basic technical data may be sent to keep the API request working correctly, such as:

  • App version
  • Device operating system type
  • A randomly generated request ID (not tied to your name or personal identity)

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, or precise real-time location unless you explicitly choose to add location tags to a rock entry, and even then, that stays on-device unless you choose to share it (e.g., via export or social sharing).

3. How We Use Information

  • To identify rocks and minerals using AI image recognition
  • To improve the accuracy of the identification model (only using anonymized data, if applicable)
  • To fix bugs and maintain app performance

4. Third-Party Services

The App may use a third-party AI/API provider to process identification requests. These providers only receive the minimum data necessary to return a result and are contractually/technically required to handle it securely. We do not sell your data to advertisers or data brokers.

5. Data Storage & Security

  • Most of your data (photos, history, collection) is stored locally on your device.
  • Data sent to our API for processing is transmitted using encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
  • We retain API request data only as long as necessary to return your result, then delete it, unless otherwise required by law.

6. Children's Privacy

GeoLens does not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what's needed to operate the app (like a photo submitted for identification). We do not require account creation with personal details. If you are a parent/guardian and believe your child has provided personal information beyond this, contact us using the information in Section 9.

7. Your Choices & Rights

Depending on your location (e.g., under GDPR in the EU/UK or CCPA in California), you may have rights to:

  • Access the data associated with your requests
  • Request deletion of data held by our API provider
  • Opt out of any optional data-sharing features

Since most data stays on your device, deleting the App or clearing its data removes the vast majority of your information immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App changes. We'll update the "Last Updated" date above when we do. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
